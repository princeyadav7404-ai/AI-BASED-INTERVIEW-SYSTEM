      About the Project

      About This Project

      AI-Based Interview Preparation System — a college project demonstrating how AI can make interview practice accessible and personalized.

      The Problem

      Many students lack access to realistic interview practice and personalized feedback. Traditional preparation is generic, one-directional, and doesn't adapt to the individual.

      The Solution

      The AI-Based Interview Preparation System provides an interactive environment where candidates can practice interviews and receive automated, structured feedback — anytime, anywhere.
